# Economy 0.0.20

- Added persistent purchase allowlists to Vaults and Tanks, configured through a Routing dialog. Filters remain saved when changing market mode and after world reloads.
- Added storage priorities for purchase delivery and sell-order supply. Higher priorities are used first; equal priorities use a deterministic dimension/position order.
- Purchase capacity checks and delivery honor the same filters and overflow order. Empty filters retain unrestricted behavior for existing storage.
- Escrow cancellation and recovery return owned goods independently of purchase filters and market modes.
- Added cross-version validation, filtered distribution, serialization, and real-server routing regressions.
